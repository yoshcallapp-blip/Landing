Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") describes prohibited uses of the Yosh Call mobile application and website (the "Service"). It supplements, and is incorporated into, our Terms of Service. By using the Service, you agree to comply with this AUP. Violations may result in content removal, suspension, permanent termination, and forfeiture of balances, and may be reported to law enforcement where required by law.

1. Prohibited Content

You may not upload, share, transmit, or solicit content that:

  • Depicts, promotes, or facilitates child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or the exploitation of minors in any way — see our Child Safety Standards
  • Is sexually explicit, obscene, or pornographic
  • Is defamatory, hateful, discriminatory, or incites violence against any individual or group based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristic
  • Threatens, harasses, bullies, stalks, or intimidates any person
  • Is fraudulent, deceptive, or misleading
  • Infringes the intellectual property, privacy, or publicity rights of others
  • Promotes illegal drugs, weapons, or other regulated or unlawful goods and services
  • Contains malware, viruses, or other harmful code

2. Prohibited Conduct

You may not use the Service to:

  • Impersonate any person, entity, or Yosh Call staff
  • Harass, threaten, or abuse other users during calls or messaging
  • Solicit personal information, off-platform contact, or off-platform payment from other users
  • Engage in escort services, prostitution, or any exchange of sexual services for compensation
  • Groom, solicit, or attempt to exploit minors
  • Use bots, scripts, macros, or other automated means to interact with the Service
  • Artificially inflate call duration, gifting, or engagement through fraud or collusion
  • Scrape, crawl, or extract data from the Service without our express written permission
  • Attempt to gain unauthorized access to accounts, systems, or networks of the Service
  • Reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble the application
  • Circumvent or interfere with any security, rate-limiting, or content-moderation measure
  • Create multiple accounts to evade a suspension or ban
  • Engage in money laundering, payment fraud, or unauthorized chargebacks
  • Violate any applicable local, national, or international law or regulation

3. Enforcement

We use a combination of automated systems, human moderation, and user reports to detect violations of this AUP. Depending on the severity and frequency of a violation, we may, at our sole discretion:

  • Remove or restrict access to the offending content
  • Issue a warning
  • Temporarily suspend the account
  • Permanently terminate the account and forfeit any pending balances
  • Report the conduct to law enforcement or relevant authorities — see our Law Enforcement Guidelines

We reserve the right to take any of the above actions without prior notice where we believe it is necessary to protect users, third parties, or the Service.

4. Reporting Violations

If you encounter content or conduct that violates this AUP, please report it using the in-app report feature on the relevant profile, call, or message, or by emailing yoshcallapp@gmail.com. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent possible.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this AUP from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised AUP.
